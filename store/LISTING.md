# Google Play listing draft

Title: Pocket Foundry: Color Sort

Short description: Sort colorful ingots, craft treasures and grow your cozy forge.

Full description:

Welcome to Pocket Foundry, your little corner of calm. Sort colorful ingots into matching molds and watch your forge come to life with every perfect set.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Easy to learn, satisfying to master. Tap an ingot mold, choose a matching or empty mold, and forge a complete color set. No timer or forced ads.

100 CAMPAIGN PUZZLES TO MASTER
Progress through 100 campaign challenges with increasing difficulty. Replay unlocked levels, improve your move count, and collect up to three stars per level.

A FRESH DAILY FORGE
Return for a new color-sorting challenge every day. Try 24 special orders, endless mastery and optional weekly goals. Unfinished daily puzzles can be resumed without losing your campaign.

MAKE IT YOURS
Earn metal and sparks, craft eight treasures and fill your workshop shelves. Unlock visual styles, or optionally purchase Foundry Plus with exclusive themes, crystal mold finishes and glow effects. Every puzzle and crafting recipe is available without payment.

PLAY OFFLINE
Your puzzle progress is saved on your device. Color symbols, undo, hints and sound settings help you enjoy the game in your own way. Google Play connectivity is required only for optional purchases and restoration.

Find your flow, one shining ingot at a time.
