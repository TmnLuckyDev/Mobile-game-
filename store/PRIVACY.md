# Pocket Foundry privacy policy

Effective at release. Developer: TmnLuckyDev. Before publication, add a verified developer support contact and effective publication date and host this policy at a public HTTPS URL.

Pocket Foundry is an offline-first puzzle game. Players do not need to create an account or provide personal details to play. Game progress, preferences and virtual rewards are stored on the device. The game does not transmit gameplay records to a developer-operated server, use advertising trackers or include an analytics SDK.

Optional Foundry Plus purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment and account information according to its own privacy policy. The game receives product information and a signed purchase record to verify, acknowledge, unlock and restore the relevant cosmetic entitlement. The verified purchase record is stored locally in app-private storage and is refreshed against Google Play when available. The developer does not receive payment card information through the game.

You may reset local game progress using Settings > Reset local progress. You may also remove local app data using Android settings or uninstall the app. Google Play purchase records remain associated with your Google Play account and may be restored. Google Play handles its records and payment disputes according to its own policies.

If the game changes to collect additional data in the future, this policy and the app's disclosures must be updated before the changed app is released.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
